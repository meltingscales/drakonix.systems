My greatest learning is that I never stopped finding the program central to my life. It always seemed to offer what I needed, long after sobriety stopped being the main challenge.

There were other challenges. I did three complete and thorough Fourth and Fifth Steps. Each taught me important lessons, and each was a very different experience. The steps are always there to help us in our process. I have friends who stopped going to meetings and moved on to what they thought were other forms of personal growth. A couple of them have had relapses, which were very costly to them. What I have found for myself is that to live the discipline of a twelve-step way of life is an enduring challenge that keeps on giving back. There is a saying in SAA that recovery does not mean that you stop having problems. Rather, you get to have problems that are not sexual ones.
