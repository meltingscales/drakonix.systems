My CB radio became my own personal play toy, and any time I was hungry, angry, lonely (that was a big one), or tired, not to mention any number of other things, I’d be out there in my car, only now I was the one doing the hunting. One night, after about a year of this, a truck driver who heard me on the radio lined up a couple of cops, set himself up as the decoy, and the cops caught me. They shamed and humiliated me, asked me if I had AIDS, laughed at me, and put me in the back of their squad car. As I got back in my car and started it up, my CB radio automatically came back on.
