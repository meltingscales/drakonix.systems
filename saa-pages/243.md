The God of my understanding requires that I take action. Just showing up was no longer sufficient. It was time to grow, to put my feet on the ground and not be a victim any longer. Again, easier said than done. But this is a program of progress, not perfection, and things, quite amazingly, have a way of turning out all right.

One of the most healing moments of my recovery came when I told a woman whom I was seeing that I was a sex addict. To my utter amazement, she wept. I have never felt such compassion, acceptance, and love. We are now married and have a wonderful daughter. I owe a lot today to friends, to actively working the Twelve Steps, having an SAA sponsor, and talking with him and other SAA members.

Just as in my addiction the quality of my life went down from being an idealistic kid to a person I could not be proud of, so it has gradually moved up in recovery. Today I can give and receive love, be challenged, have friends I can trust, have ideals, and, best of all, feel integrated and whole.
