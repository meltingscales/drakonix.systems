He added how this had helped him to share with his wife about a sexual matter that he had been struggling with, and how this had resulted in a closer relationship for them.

My road to reconciliation has not been easy. I have found that most things of great value in life are not easy. Pains still exist for my wife, my daughters, and me. Still, my wife and I have never been closer, and I have never felt closer to God or to other people than I have following this difficult part of my journey.

I still have to be aware of my pride. I often want to run my own show, and that can get me into trouble. Too often, I want to make my own plans for God, and some of these may seem good. I need to remember that He wants me a lot more than He wants my plans. There is a saying, “If you want to make God laugh, tell Him your plans.” If God has me rather than my plans, then God’s plans can work through me. In living a more authentic life, I feel God has more of me.
