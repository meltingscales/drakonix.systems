In the fall of 1979, I came back to the town where I had gone to grad school, still not having finished my dissertation. I got a full-time job, and in the evenings I was more interested in hanging at the bars than in working on my paper. Eventually I discovered a much bigger city in the same state where I discerned that the acting-out opportunities were greater, so I moved there.
