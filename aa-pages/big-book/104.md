       If you are satisfied that he is a real alcoholic, begin to dwell on the hopeless
     feature of the malady. Show him, from your own experience, how the queer
     mental condition surrounding that first drink prevents normal functioning of
     the will power. Don’t at this stage refer to this book, unless he has seen it and
     wishes to discuss it. And be careful not to brand him an alcoholic. Let him
     draw his own conclusion. If he sticks to the idea that he can still control his
     drinking, tell him that possibly he can—if he is not too alcoholic. But insist
     that if he is severely afflicted, there may be little chance he can recover by
     himself.
