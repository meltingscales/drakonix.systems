       We have shown how we got out from under. You say: ”Yes, I’m willing. But
     am I to be consigned to a life where I shall be stupid, boring and glum, like
     some righteous people I see? I know I must get along without liquor, but how
     can I? Have you a sufficient substitute? ”
       Yes, there is a substitute, and it is vastly more than that. It is a Fellowship
     in Alcoholics Anonymous. There you will find release from care, boredom,
     and worry. Your imagination will be fired. Life will mean something at last.
     The most satisfactory years of your existence lie ahead. Thus we find The
     Fellowship, and so will you.
