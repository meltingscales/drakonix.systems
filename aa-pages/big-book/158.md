  We suggest you draw the book to the attention of the doctor who is to attend
your patient during treatment. If the book is read the moment the patient is
able, while acutely depressed, realization of his condition may come to him.
  We hope the doctor will tell the patient the truth about his condition, what-
ever that happens to be. When the man is presented with this volume it is best
that no one tell him he must abide by its suggestions. The man must decide for
himself.
