         Finally I very self-consciously and briefly asked God to show me how to do
      what He wanted me to do. My prayer was just about as weak and helpless a
      thing as one could imagine, but it taught me how to open my mouth and pray
      earnestly and sincerely. However, I had not quite made the grade. I was full of
