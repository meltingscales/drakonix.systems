          officers from Plattsburg were assigned, and we were flattered when the
    first citizens took us to their homes, making us feel heroic. Here was love,

    last, and in the midst of the excitement I discovered liquor. I forgot the strong
    warnings and the prejudices of my people concerning drink. In time we sailed
    for ”Over There.“ I was very lonely and again turned to alcohol.

           ”Here lies a Hampshire Grenadier
           Who caught his death
