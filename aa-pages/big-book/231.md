        Well—well—look who’s here! Good old Charlie! It’s great to see him. How’s

      cheap. Yet I am fairly steady when I go to bed.
        Morning comes. I feel horribly. A little drink will put me on my feet. But it
      takes others to keep me there.
        I see some prospects. I am too miserable to care if they give me an order or
      not. My breath would knock out a mule, I learn from a friend. Back at the hotel
