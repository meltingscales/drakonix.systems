   In this way we tried to shape a sane and sound ideal for our future sex life.
We subjected each relation to this test-was it selfish or not? We asked God to
mold our ideals and help us to live up to them. We remembered always that our
sex powers were God-given, and therefore good, neither to be used lightly or
selfishly nor to be despised and loathed. Whatever our ideal turns out to be, we
must be willing to grow toward it. We must be willing to make amends where
we have done harm, provided that we do not bring about still more harm in so
doing. In other words, we treat sex as we would any other problem. In medita-
tion, we ask God what we should do about each specific matter. The right
answer will come, if we want it.
   God alone can judge our sex situation. Counsel with persons is often desir-
able, but we let God be the final judge. We realize that some people are as
