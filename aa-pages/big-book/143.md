     preferable to that.
        Those of us who have spent much time in the world of spiritual make-be-
     lieve have eventually seen the childishness of it. This dream world has been
     replaced by a great sense of purpose, accompanied by a growing conscious-
     ness of the power of God in our lives. We have come to believe He would like
     us to keep our heads in the clouds with Him, but that our feet ought to be
     firmly planted on earth, nevertheless. That is where our fellow travelers are,
     and that is where our work must be done. These are the realities for us. We
     have found nothing incompatible between a powerful spiritual experience, and
     a life of sane and happy usefulness.
