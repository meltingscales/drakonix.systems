   Thus I drifted. Breaking promises to my wife, my mother, and a host of
other relatives and friends who stood more from me and tried harder than
humans should be expected to, to help me.
   I always seemed to pick the most inopportune time for a binge. An im-
portant business deal to be closed might find me in another city. Once when
entrusted to purchase for a large customer, I agreed to meet his representa-
tive in New York. I spent the time waiting for a train in a bar; arrived in
New York tight; stayed tight the week; and came home by a route twice the
distance from New York.
