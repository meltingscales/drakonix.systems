”Then they outlined the spiritual answer and program of action which a hun-
     dred of them had followed successfully. Though I had been only a nominal
     churchman, their proposals were not, intellectually, hard to swallow. But the
     program of action, though entirely sensible, was pretty drastic. It meant I would
     have to throw several lifelong conceptions out of the window. That was not
     easy. But the moment I made up my mind to go through with the process, I had
     the curious feeling that my alcoholic condition was relieved, as in fact it proved
     to be.
       ”Quite as important was the discovery that spiritual principles would solve
     all my problems. I have since been brought into a way of living infinitely more
     satisfying and, I hope, more useful than the life I lived before. My old manner
     of life was by no means a bad one, but I would not exchange its best moments
     for the worst I have now. I would not go back to it even if I could.“
       Fred’s story speaks for itself. We hope it strikes home to thousands like him.
     He had felt only the first nip of the wringer. Most alcoholics have to be pretty
     badly mangled before they really commence to solve their problems.
