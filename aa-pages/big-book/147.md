   But this does not mean that we disregard human health measures. God has
abundantly supplied this world with fine doctors, psychologists, and practi-
tioners of various kinds. Do not hesitate to take your health problems to such
persons. Most of them give freely of themselves, that their fellows may en-
joy sound minds and bodies. Try to remember that though God has wrought
miracles among us, we should never belittle a good doctor or psychiatrist.
Their services are often indispensable in treating a newcomer and following
his case afterward.

       One of the many doctors who had the opportunity of reading this book in
     manuscript form told us that the use of sweets was often helpful, of course
     depending upon a doctor’s advice. He thought all alcoholics should constantly
     have chocolate available for its quick energy value at times of fatigue. He added
     that occasionally in the night a vague craving arose which would be satisfied
     by candy. Many of us have noticed a tendency to eat sweets and have found
     this practice beneficial.
