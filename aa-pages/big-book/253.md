  Within sixty days, after leaving the hospital the second time, I was pounding
at his door, willing to do anything to conquer the vicious thing that had con-
quered me.
  The point I hope I have made is—even a man with everything from the ma-
terial standpoint, a man with tremendous pride and the will power to function
in all ordinary circumstances can become an alcoholic and find himself as
hopeless and helpless as the man who has a multitude of worries and troubles.
