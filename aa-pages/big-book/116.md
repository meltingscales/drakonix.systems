                                TO WIVES
W    ITH few exceptions, our book thus far has spoken of men. But what we
     have said applies quite as much to women. Our activities in behalf of
women who drink are on the increase. There is every evidence that women
regain their health as readily as men if they try our suggestions.
  But for every man who drinks others are involved—the wife who trembles
in fear of the next debauch; the mother and father who see their son wasting
away.

       Among us are wives, relatives, and friends whose problem has been solved,
     as well as some who have not yet found a happy solution. We want the wives of
     Alcoholics Anonymous to address the wives of men who drink too much. What
     they say will apply to nearly everyone bound by ties of blood or affection to an
     alcoholic.
       As wives of Alcoholics Anonymous, we would like you to feel that we un-
     derstand as perhaps few can. We want to analyze mistakes we have made. We
     want to leave you with the feeling that no situation is too difficult and no un-
     happiness too great to be overcome.
