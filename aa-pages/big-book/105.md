     believe in a Power greater than himself and that he live by spiritual principles.
        When dealing with such a person, you had better use everyday language to
     describe spiritual principles. There is no use arousing any prejudice he may have
     against certain theological terms and conceptions, about which he may already
     be confused. Don’t raise such issues, no matter what your own convictions are.
        Your prospect may belong to a religious denomination. His religious educa-
