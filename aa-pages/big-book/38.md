   Upon hearing this, our friend was somewhat relieved, for he reflected that,
after all, he was a good church member. This hope, however, was destroyed by
the doctor’s telling him that his religious convictions were very good, but that
in his case they did not spell the necessary vital spiritual experience.
   Here was the terrible dilemma in which our friend found himself when he
had the extraordinary experience, which as we have already told you, made
him a free man.
   We, in our turn, sought the same escape, with all the desperation of drown-
ing men. What seemed at first a flimsy reed, has proved to be the loving and
powerful hand of God. A new life has been given us or, if you prefer, ”a design
for living“ that really works.
