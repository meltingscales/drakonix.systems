   I was getting along fairly well with my party drinking for quite some time
however, but eventually I began to visit beer joints in between parties. A night
or so a week in a joint, and a party or so a week at home or with friends, along
with a little lone drinking, soon had me preparing for the existence of a top
flight drunkard.
