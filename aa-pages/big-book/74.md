of our fellows and they retaliate. Sometimes they hurt us, seemingly, without
provocation, but we invariably find that at some time in the past we have made
decisions based on self, which later placed us in a position to be hurt.
  So our troubles, we think, are basically of our own making. They arise out of
ourselves, and the alcoholic is an extreme example of self-will run riot, though
he usually doesn’t think so. Above everything, we alcoholics must be rid of
this selfishness. We must, or it kills us! God makes that possible. And there
often seems no way of entirely getting rid of self without Him. Many of us had
