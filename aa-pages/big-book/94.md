any sort, we have often found this the best course to take.
   Our design for living is not a one-way street. It is as good for the wife as for
the husband. If we can forget, so can she. It is better, however, that one does
not needlessly name a person upon whom she can vent jealousy.
   Perhaps there are some cases where the utmost frankness is demanded. No
outsider can appraise such an intimate situation. It may be both will decide
that the way of good sense and loving kindness is to let bygones be bygones.
Each might pray about it, having the other one’s happiness uppermost in mind.
Keep it always in sight that we deal with that most terrible human emotion—
jealousy. Good generalship may decide that the problem be attacked on the
flank, rather than risk a face-to-face combat.
   Should we have no such complication, there is plenty we should do at home.
